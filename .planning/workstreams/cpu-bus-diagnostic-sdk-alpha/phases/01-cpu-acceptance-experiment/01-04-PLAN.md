## Artifacts this phase produces

New `tools/cpu/acceptance.py` is host-only standard-library Python, with `collect`, `self-test`, and `verify` subcommands. `collect --build-dir build/cpu-final` writes `experiments/cpu/acceptance-results.json` after actual commands; `verify --require-accepted` verifies without executing or rewriting results. New `experiments/cpu/REVIEW.md` is an independent review receipt. CTest registers `cpu_acceptance_controls` for synthetic test subjects and `cpu_acceptance` for the real receipt; both use a timeout and nonempty case counts. The collector runs guest/source suites with acceptance tests excluded to avoid recursion. It never infers execution from build success.

Result records include source revision and content digest; frozen-contract revision; vendor, generator, configuration and fixture identities; exact compiler/SDK/OS/architecture; commands; expected/observed case counts; output digests; distinct pass/fail/skipped/unsupported/unknown outcomes; limitation/reason; review identity tied to evaluated source; decision and `phase_status` (`ACCEPTED` or `GAPS_FOUND`). Store repository-relative public-safe paths. Keep volatile capture timestamps outside the canonical sorted decision payload. Document the entire required denominator, including unsupported lanes and capabilities. Current-source matching covers all runtime/build/test/tool/fixture inputs; evidence files themselves are excluded from the content digest to avoid self-reference, and later documentation-only commits do not pretend to rerun runtime tests.

## Early rejection path

This plan is the normal final path after plans 01–03. At any earlier fatal counterexample or cap violation, immediately append a rejection/defer receipt to ACCEPTANCE.md and report `GAPS_FOUND`; the receipt can be written without a working build or this collector. Preserve available hashes, failed commands, minimized original-input counterexamples, remaining missing CPU evidence and bounded replacement/replanning recommendation. Do not execute later dependent implementation plans merely to complete their checkboxes. Once the collector exists it must ingest that rejection without granting admission. No alternate CPU or scope change is implemented by this plan.
