Purpose: T-04-11 is the last open high disclosure threat in the qualification runner; a raw traceback carries absolute paths past every public sink. The provenance gate currently accepts any ancestor commit and never parses `reviewed_revision`, so the dispositions it guards are not even ordered after the review that produced them.

Design choice (Claude's discretion, adversarially reviewed): the R2 fix text suggests requiring each disposition revision to equal `reviewed_revision`. That cannot hold here: 04-REVIEW.md reviewed through 867f317, while the dispositions cite later contained verdict reviews (04-34 `45952598202a`, 04-36 `323fa1453329`), which by construction postdate the review. The binding implemented is therefore a revision window — verdict revision descends from `reviewed_revision` and is an ancestor of head. That window proves ordering only; it does not bind a verdict to the cited files at that revision. The T-04-38 claim is narrowed accordingly: docs/testing.md (04-42) states exactly that guarantee and its limit, 04-40 rewrites the T-04-38 register row to the same narrowed claim and keeps it open, and file freshness since a verdict comes from the focused re-review, not this gate (the verifier allowed "narrow the docs claim or enforce unchanged files"; enforcing unchanged files would fail every current row the moment these fixes land, before any re-review can run).
