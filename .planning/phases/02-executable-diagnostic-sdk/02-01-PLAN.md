Planner checked register fields, absolute-long extensions, source prefix ending before 0x100a, high-word preservation requiring explicit D1/D2 clearing, and instruction widths. Selected public shape: gn_instance opaque handle; gn_create, gn_load, gn_reset, gn_run, gn_observe, gn_unload, gn_destroy, gn_status_string. Fixed-width statuses and gn_run_result carry requested/elapsed/overshoot/instructions/reason/fault_pc/instruction_register. gn_observations exposes only arithmetic_result, initialized_result, bss_result and readiness, never complete CPU registers. gn_manifest has profile/version, region count and descriptors containing guest base, mapped length, source pointer/source length and ROM/RAM role. Cap at 2 regions and 1 MiB total copied seed plus live storage; require supported diagnostic mapping ROM [0,512), RAM [0x1000,0x2000), SSP exclusive top 0x2000 and aligned mapped PC. These concrete caps/layouts are delegated discretion, not preexisting user-approved values.
